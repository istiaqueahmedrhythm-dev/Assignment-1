Frequently Asked Questions

Are tickets refundable?

Yes, standard and pro tickets are fully refundable up to 30 days before the event. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague or friend without any additional fees.

Do you offer student discounts?

We do! Students with a valid .edu email address or current university ID can apply for a 50% discount on the Standard pass. Please email support@devconf.com to receive your promo code.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes. All main stage keynotes and technical tracks are recorded. Standard pass holders get 30-day digital access, while Pro and Team pass holders receive lifetime access to the recordings.

Can I upgrade my ticket later?

Yes, you can upgrade from Standard to Pro at any time before October 1st, subject to availability. You will only need to pay the difference in the ticket price.

Are meals included in the ticket price?

Yes, all ticket tiers include catered networking lunches on all three days, as well as unlimited coffee, tea, and snacks during the scheduled breaks.

Is there a Code of Conduct?

Absolutely. DevConf is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone. All attendees, speakers, and sponsors must agree to our Code of Conduct upon registration.

Do you offer virtual or online-only tickets?

Currently, DevConf 2026 is an in-person only event to maximize the value of hands-on workshops and networking. However, digital recordings are included with all physical tickets.

How do I book a private workshop session?

Private workshop bookings are exclusive to the Team pass. Once your Team pass is purchased, our event coordinators will reach out directly to schedule your dedicated session.
